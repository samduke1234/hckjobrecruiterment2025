Recruitment Office — Candidate Information

Important notice for applicants who applied in 2024 and 2025

⚠️

Temporary stay order — recruitment delayed for 3 months

A temporary stay order issued by the court has delayed the recruitment process for candidates who applied in 2024 and 2025. Any official updates will be published on this website. Please read the information below for next steps and FAQs.

What this means

Because of the court's temporary stay order, active recruitment activities (including interviews, offer issuance, and onboarding) for the specified applicant pools are paused. The stay order duration is three months from the court's order. This page will be updated as soon as the recruitment office receives official instructions or a court order lifting/modifying the stay.

Who is affected

Applicant cohorts
All candidates who submitted applications during the 2024 and 2025 recruitment cycles (including online, offline, and referral submissions).
Recruitment activities paused
Interviews, written tests, group discussions, offer letters, and onboarding scheduling are included in the pause.

Official updates

The recruitment office will publish official updates and timelines on this website only. Do not rely on unofficial messages (email chains, social media posts) that claim to accelerate or change the process. If there is an authorized change, it will appear here along with an official PDF or press-release style notice.

Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

Q. When exactly does the three-month delay start?

A. The three-month delay counts from the date the court's temporary stay order was issued. The recruitment office will publish the order date and the corresponding end date of the stay on this page when that information is available.

Q. Will I lose my application or priority?

A. No. All applications submitted for the 2024 and 2025 cycles remain on file. The pause is procedural; we will not discard applications because of the stay.

Q. Can I contact someone directly?

A. For general questions, use the contact form or the official email listed below. Please avoid contacting individual recruiters asking for exceptions — official updates will be published here.

Q. How will I be notified when recruitment resumes?

A. Notifications will appear on this website and — if you subscribe — via the optional email list. We will also publish a downloadable PDF containing the revised timetable and next steps.
